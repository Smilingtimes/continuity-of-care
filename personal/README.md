# Personal Layer (Optional)

This folder holds deeper personal context — the files an AI collaborator reads
only when the task genuinely involves you personally: career planning, writing
in your voice, life reflection, or values-sensitive decisions.

**It is optional and read on demand.** The workspace works without it. Fill in
only what is useful to share with an AI.

## Privacy rules

- Assume anything here may be pasted into an AI session. Write accordingly.
- Never store passwords, tokens, government identifiers, or other people's
  private information.
- If you publish your workspace, delete or empty this folder first.
- For genuinely private archives, use encrypted, access-controlled storage kept
  separate from files shared with AI agents.

## Files

- `PROFILE.md` — who you are in full depth.
- `PRINCIPLES.md` — purpose, values, and philosophy.
- `TIMELINE.md` — verified personal history.
- `LIFE-VISION.md` — the future you are moving toward.
