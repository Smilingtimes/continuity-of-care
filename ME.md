## How to read my files

Information is labeled so an AI does not turn guesses into facts:

- **I said:** something you have directly expressed.
- **Working interpretation:** a reasonable reading you still need to confirm.
- **Open:** something only you can properly answer.
- **Private boundary:** information that should not be stored here.

An AI should use these documents to understand and support you — not to
manipulate you, make intimate assumptions, diagnose you, or make major life
decisions for you.
